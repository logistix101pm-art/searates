How we make money, and what money cannot buy here

By SeaRates · Updated 29 July 2026 · Every figure carries its unit, its reading date and a linked source · Send a correction

⚑ Disclosure

No outbound link on this site carries a referral arrangement today: nothing here earns SeaRates a fee when a reader clicks through. Referral links are the entire commercial model when they appear, and the terms below are the standing ones. Ranking order, inclusion in a list, and every published figure are decided before and independently of any payment. No company has paid to appear, to move position or to be removed, and none can.

Why this is stated so plainly

The Federal Trade Commission requires a material connection between a publisher and a seller to be disclosed clearly and conspicuously, close to the recommendation rather than buried. This page is the full statement; the short form appears on every page that carries such a link.

There is a second reason, which is self-interested. A site whose whole claim is that figures are checkable cannot ask readers to take its independence on trust. Publishing the arrangement in full is the only version of that claim that survives being checked.

What to do if you think a finding was bought

Say so, and name the page. A correction that arrives with a source is published with the date it was made, and the original figure stays visible in the change note. That applies to a disputed finding exactly as it applies to a disputed number.

Nothing on this site is a recommendation to book with any company. A licence is a floor, not an endorsement: it excludes the counterparty that cannot be complained to, which is a smaller claim than a badge implies and a more reliable one.
