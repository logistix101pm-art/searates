What "ranked by visibility" means, and what it does not

Two of the lists above are ordered by how many of the measured queries name each company. That is a real, repeatable measurement, and it is a measurement of publishing, not of service: it says a company is written about often, and says nothing about whether it answers the phone, quotes honestly or holds the licence it implies. It is labelled that way on the page, and the credential column next to each name is the part you can check yourself in the federal registers.

The three lists carrying no ordering carry none because the underlying data does not exist in public form. Rather than score on adjectives, each of those pages publishes the four rows a verifiable ranking would need and marks which ones SeaRates holds. When the missing rows are collected, the ordering appears and the date it was collected appears with it.
