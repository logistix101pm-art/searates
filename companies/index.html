What a card carries, and what it still refuses to carry

Eleven of the fields on a card are regulatory — licence and organization number, registration, each bond with its surety and effective date, the renewal date and the qualifying individual — and not one of them can be derived from search results. Every regulatory field published here comes from the FMC OTI register and from nothing else: no figure is carried over from FMCSA SAFER or from a state business registry, because neither was read for these records. A card prints what was read and omits what was not — a blank line pretending to be data is worse than an absent one.

Since 12.09.2026 a card also carries a reputation score, computed from independently-sourced platforms (Trustpilot, the Better Business Bureau) and disclosure checks — method published in full. That score replaces the promise this hub made until then, that no card would ever carry a score: the promise is retired, not quietly broken — this line says so, and every card states the change too. Why the register cannot be pulled automatically

The FMC search application is an ASP.NET form: the filter and the paging both run as posts, and query parameters are ignored. SeaRates confirmed this on 07/28/2026 — a name filter and a page parameter both returned the unfiltered first page. Reading 3,264 rows twenty at a time is not a reasonable thing to do to a federal server, and the full export sits behind a button that only a person can press.

That is also the reason the counts on this site carry reading dates rather than being treated as settled: the registry's own web application showed 3,273 forwarder rows on 07/26/2026 and 3,264 on 07/28/2026 — nine fewer in two days, and titled "Trade Names" rather than organisations in the first place. SeaRates instead reads the Commission's full data export, current as of 28/07/2026: 9,795 organisations, of which 5,205 carry an active licence. A figure from this register without its date and its unit is not a fact about the register.
