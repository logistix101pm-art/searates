What this role means for your shipment

NVOCC. Issues its own bill of lading, publishes its own tariff and takes on carrier liability for the cargo.

Ocean freight forwarder. Acts as your agent. Issues no bill of lading of its own and does not take on carrier liability.

This entity holds both roles, so the register does not settle which one applies to your booking. The document does: whoever is named as carrier on the bill of lading is the carrier for that shipment. The distinction, and the bond behind each role, is set out in is an NVOCC the same as a freight forwarder.
