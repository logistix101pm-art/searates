The ordering is by expected cost, not by frequency. The free-time clock is first because it accrues daily and starts before most importers are told.

The two that are pure process

Free time. Three to seven days at most US terminals, and it starts when the container is available rather than when you hear about it. File the entry before arrival, confirm the last free day in writing at booking, and book drayage as soon as the vessel schedule firms.

The defective invoice. Since 28 May 2024 a US demurrage or detention invoice must reach the billed party within 30 days and carry 20 elements specified under 46 CFR 541.6. An invoice missing a required element carries no obligation to pay — which is a stronger position than any discount, and 7 of 86 measured pages mention it.

The two that are pure arithmetic

Scope. Port-to-port against door-to-door is not a price difference, it is a different product. Two quotes are comparable only after both are normalised, and the fastest way to normalise is to ask for the exclusions rather than the inclusions.

The LCL boundary. The crossover to a full container usually sits at 13 to 15 CBM and moves with density, because LCL bills on weight or measure, whichever is higher. Adding the destination CFS charge at $15-165 per CBM moves the boundary again, downward.

The two that are pure diligence

The licence. Search the entity that issues the bill of lading in the FMC register, by company name and by trade name. It is free and takes about two minutes, and 4 of 245 measured pages point readers at it.

The index. A composite averages many shippers, volumes and contract positions. Drewry assessed a 40-foot container at $4,374 on 07/23/2026, down 4% on the week — useful as direction, useless as a quote, and unreadable without its date.

Frequently asked questions

What are common freight mistakes?

Missing the free-time window, comparing quotes of different scopes, ignoring the destination CFS charge on LCL, skipping the licence check, treating an index as a quote, and paying a defective demurrage invoice.

What is the most expensive freight mistake?

Losing the free-time window. Published demurrage runs $75-300 per container per day, escalates in tiers, and the clock starts when the box is available rather than when you are notified.

How do I compare two freight quotes properly?

Normalise the scope first, by asking each forwarder for the exclusions in writing. Two quotes on different scopes cannot be compared however precise the numbers look.

Can I refuse a demurrage invoice?

If it arrived more than 30 days after the charge stopped accruing, or is missing any of the 20 elements required under 46 CFR 541.6, it carries no obligation to pay.

Is checking a licence really necessary?

It takes about two minutes and is free. Two of the 245 pages measured show a licence number, so the market will not do it for you.
