Those are ranges published by sellers, not medians from a quote array, and the spread inside each is mostly volume and destination rather than mover. Volume, not weight

A domestic US move is quoted on weight. An ocean move is quoted on volume, because a container has a fixed cubic capacity and the ship charges for the box. The switch catches people out in both directions: light bulky furniture costs more than its weight suggests, and dense small items cost less.

One cubic metre is 35.3 cubic feet. Every quote worth comparing states the volume it was built on, and a surveyed volume beats an estimated one every time.

Sixty cents a pound is not insurance

The standard liability included in most international moving contracts is released value at 60 cents per pound per article. A 40-pound television is covered for $24 regardless of what it cost.

Full value protection is the alternative, priced separately, and it obliges the mover to repair, replace or pay the current value of the item. The gap between the two is the largest single misunderstanding in the segment, and it is settled by one line in the contract.

A duty exemption for used household goods exists in most markets and is automatic in none: it usually turns on how long the goods were owned and how long you have lived abroad, and it is claimed with documents rather than granted by default.

Binding, non-binding and the 110% rule

A binding estimate fixes the price for the inventory surveyed. A non-binding estimate does not, and under the US rule the mover may collect no more than 110% of a non-binding estimate at delivery, with the balance due within 30 days.

That rule protects the delivery day and does not cap the final bill. The way to make either estimate meaningful is the same: a proper survey, in person or on video, of everything that is actually going.

Frequently asked questions

What is the average cost of an international move?

Published bands ran $1,500-3,000 for a shared container and $6,000-12,000 for a three-bedroom household as read on 07/25-26/2026. These are seller ranges, not medians, and destination charges sit outside most of them.

How much does it cost to move furniture internationally?

It is priced by volume in cubic metres rather than by weight. A studio at 10-20 CBM sits in the shared-container band; a three-bedroom household at 30-50 CBM usually needs a box of its own.

What does 60 cents a pound cover?

Released value liability pays 60 cents per pound per article, so a 40-pound item is covered for $24 whatever it cost. Full value protection is a separate, priced option.

Is a binding estimate better?

It fixes the price for the surveyed inventory, which removes the delivery-day argument. A non-binding estimate is capped at 110% collectible on delivery under the US rule, with the balance due within 30 days.

Do I pay duty on my own belongings?

Usually not, but the exemption is claimed rather than granted. Most markets require the goods to have been owned for a minimum period and the owner to have lived abroad for one, evidenced with documents at clearance.
